# Team Pipeline Orchestration

## Overview
Deploys and supervises collaborative multi-agent topologies where a team leader decomposes complex objectives, assigns sub-tasks to specialist member agents (such as analysts, coders, and critics), and aggregates results into a unified outcome.

## Key Capabilities
- Leader-led task decomposition and iterative delegation.
- Round-robin and turn-limited group debate protocols.
- Structured synthesis and automated majority or unanimous consensus checking.
